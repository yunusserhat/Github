The building and road geometry databases, including the enriched building
database in data/buildings.geojson, are made available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Source information and the exact Overture release, extraction checksum and
upstream source datasets are recorded in data/metadata.json. No building
height is asserted to be a surveyed measurement solely because it is present
in a source field. Model perception scores describe adjacent street context.
